The spec

What the job is

PillThe header Trash pill gains View and 📂 Open in Finder. Empty stays, still behind its confirm
Trash listTrashed takes newest first, grouped by day: recorded time, length, size, ▶ preview
TranscriptsShown under their recording. Transcripts whose recording isn't in the trash get their own group
RestoreOne click moves the take to the watch folder (watchDirectory, today ~/ecamm) under its original Ecamm name. It then appears in Incoming like any new take
Its transcriptsBrought back with it, or the message says plainly they were left behind
ClashIf the same name is already in Incoming, nothing moves and the message says why. Never overwrite

proofServer test: restore moves the file out of -trash/ into the watch folder, the trash count drops by one, a clash moves nothing. UI test: the list shows a trashed take with its time and length
